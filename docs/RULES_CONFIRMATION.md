# Thulla rules — please confirm

The two reference videos could not be watched or transcribed while building
this project, so the rules below are **assumed common Thulla/Bhabhi rules**,
NOT verified against the videos. Each item maps to a field in
`thulla/engine/rules.py`. Tell us what differs and it is a one-line change.

| # | Topic | Currently implemented (ASSUMED) |
|---|-------|---------------------------------|
| 1 | Deck | One standard 52-card deck, Ace high |
| 2 | Players | 2–8 |
| 3 | Dealing | All cards dealt one at a time; some players get 1 extra if 52 doesn't divide evenly |
| 4 | Start | Holder of the Ace of Spades leads, and that Ace must be the first card |
| 5 | Direction | Clockwise (seat order) |
| 6 | Following suit | Must follow the lead suit if you hold it |
| 7 | Creating a Thulla | A player who cannot follow suit plays any card → the trick ends immediately |
| 8 | Who collects | The player with the highest card of the lead suit picks up the whole pile and leads next |
| 9 | Clean round | If everyone follows suit, the pile is discarded; highest lead-suit card leads next |
| 10 | Winning | Players are safe when their hand is empty (after the trick resolves) |
| 11 | Losing | The last player holding cards is the Thulla (loser) |
| 12 | Scoring | Win/loss/Thulla counts in basic statistics; no points system |
| 13 | Round end | Game ends when one player still holds cards |
| 14 | Player-count variations | None |
